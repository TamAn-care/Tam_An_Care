TAM AN CARE V7.4.3
STEP 7S — PERSONAL HYGIENE / BATHING / TOILETING / CONTINENCE MANAGEMENT

PURPOSE

Provide an auditable operational domain for personal hygiene,
bathing, toileting, continence support and dignity-preserving
daily personal care for residents.

DOMAIN FLOW

Resident
-> Human Personal-Care Need / Plan
-> Schedule
-> Human Assignment
-> Human Acceptance
-> Safety / Privacy Readiness
-> Care Performed
-> Completed / Refused / Missed / Held / Cancelled
-> Toileting / Continence Observation
-> Personal-Care Assistance Record
-> Human Escalation when required
-> Audit


CORE PRINCIPLES

1. Personal care is performed and documented by authorized humans.
2. Privacy, dignity and resident consent are first-class safety requirements.
3. Refusal must be documented, respected and escalated when appropriate.
4. AI never records that personal care was performed.
5. AI never fabricates resident consent.
6. AI never fabricates toileting, continence or hygiene observations.
7. AI never autonomously changes medication.
8. AI never diagnoses infection, incontinence type or dermatological disease.
9. AI never autonomously changes clinical treatment.
10. Cross-domain actions require explicit human-authorized workflow.
11. Verified care records are never silently overwritten.
12. Corrections use append-only amendment history.
13. Competing state transitions use transaction and row locking.
14. Failed mutation = zero business mutation + zero audit.
15. autonomousClinicalAction = false.
