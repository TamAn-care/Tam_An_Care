STEP 7Z — FALL / MOBILITY / TRANSFER SAFETY CONTRACT

FALL != INJURY DIAGNOSIS

A fall event may trigger human assessment.

It does not automatically establish:

- fracture
- head injury
- concussion
- neurological injury
- internal injury


NEAR FALL != FALL

Near-fall history must remain distinguishable from confirmed fall history.


MOBILITY RIGHTS / DIGNITY

Fall prevention must not automatically remove mobility rights.

No automatic:
- bed confinement
- chair confinement
- physical restraint
- chemical restraint
- family restriction
- activity restriction


TRANSFER SAFETY

Transfer-assistance requirements must be human-defined.

AI may flag possible mismatch but cannot change official assistance level.


POST-FALL SAFETY

Post-fall factual observation may include observable facts.

Diagnosis and treatment remain human clinical decisions.


MEDICATION BOUNDARY

Step 7Z cannot:
- create medication order
- alter dose
- select PRN
- hold medication
- administer medication


TRANSFER-TO-HOSPITAL BOUNDARY

Step 7Z cannot autonomously decide:
- ambulance activation
- emergency department transfer
- hospital admission


FAIL-CLOSED

Unauthorized mutation:
zero business mutation
zero audit

Wrong owner / reviewer:
zero business mutation
zero audit

AUTONOMOUS RESTRAINT = FALSE
AUTONOMOUS MEDICATION ACTION = FALSE
AUTONOMOUS HOSPITAL TRANSFER = FALSE
AUTONOMOUS CLINICAL ACTION = FALSE
