STEP 7Z — AUDIT CONTRACT

Every successful official mutation requires audit.

Audit:
- append-only
- attributable
- immutable
- uniquely sequenced by entity
- same transaction as business mutation

Preserve where applicable:
- assessor
- verifier
- plan owner
- reviewer
- fall-event reporter
- assigned owner
- escalation reviewer
- prevention-action authorizer

Failed mutation = zero audit.
Unauthorized mutation = zero audit.
Wrong-owner mutation = zero audit.
Wrong-reviewer mutation = zero audit.
Losing incompatible concurrent mutation = zero audit.

AI / SYSTEM cannot impersonate human actor.
