STEP 7T — HUMAN AUTHORIZATION MATRIX

GENERAL RULE
Official mutation requires attributable HUMAN actor.

AI = advisory only.
SYSTEM = infrastructure only.
AI/SYSTEM cannot impersonate a human actor.

HUMAN CARE STAFF
- record observable cognitive observation
- record behavioral episode
- record wandering/exit-seeking observation
- document support response
- acknowledge assigned workflow when authorized
- cannot diagnose dementia/delirium
- cannot prescribe medication through this domain
- cannot authorize restraint through this domain

NURSE / AUTHORIZED CLINICAL HUMAN
- review observations
- verify records when policy allows
- initiate human clinical escalation
- document response
- participate in post-event review
- no authority is implied beyond organizational credentialing

SUPERVISOR / CARE MANAGER
- assign workflow owner
- review behavioral support plan
- activate/retire support plan when authorized
- assign escalation reviewer
- perform governance review

AUTHORIZED HUMAN REVIEWER
- accept assigned escalation
- review evidence
- resolve escalation
- participate in post-event review

INDEPENDENT HUMAN CHECKER
Required wherever local policy marks an intervention or decision as
requiring independent review.

AI / SYSTEM
DENIED:
- official cognitive diagnosis
- dementia diagnosis
- delirium diagnosis
- psychiatric diagnosis
- official behavioral episode creation
- official wandering/elopement event creation
- support-plan activation
- assignment
- acknowledgement
- record verification
- escalation acceptance
- escalation resolution
- medication mutation
- restraint decision
- sedation decision
- isolation/seclusion decision
- transfer decision
- emergency-service decision
- incident creation
- care-action creation
- care-task creation
