STEP 7W — INFECTION SAFETY CONTRACT

OBSERVATION != DIAGNOSIS

No symptom observation, vital-sign observation, exposure,
or cluster signal independently establishes infection.


NO AUTONOMOUS DIAGNOSIS

The system must not autonomously diagnose:

- infection
- sepsis
- COVID-19
- influenza
- pneumonia
- urinary tract infection
- gastroenteritis
- wound infection


NO HARDCODED TREATMENT THRESHOLD

No temperature, pulse, respiratory rate, oxygen saturation,
symptom count, or duration automatically triggers:

- antibiotic
- antiviral
- medication
- isolation
- quarantine
- transfer
- emergency service


PRECAUTION SAFETY

Precautions require authorized human confirmation.

AI may suggest review.

AI cannot:
- activate precaution
- discontinue precaution
- impose isolation
- impose quarantine


MEDICATION BOUNDARY

Step 7W cannot:

- prescribe antibiotic
- prescribe antiviral
- select antimicrobial
- change dose
- discontinue medication
- administer medication


EMERGENCY BOUNDARY

Potentially serious patterns may generate an advisory signal.

Emergency treatment, transfer, ambulance activation,
or hospital referral remains a human-authorized decision.


FAIL-CLOSED

Unauthorized mutation:
zero business mutation
zero audit

Invalid lifecycle mutation:
zero business mutation
zero audit

Wrong reviewer:
zero business mutation
zero audit

AUTONOMOUS CLINICAL ACTION = FALSE
