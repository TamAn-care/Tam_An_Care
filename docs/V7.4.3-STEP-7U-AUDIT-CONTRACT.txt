STEP 7U — AUDIT CONTRACT

Every successful official mutation requires audit.

AUDIT REQUIREMENTS

1. Same database transaction as business mutation.
2. Append-only.
3. Attributable actor.
4. Actor role recorded.
5. Entity identity recorded.
6. Event type recorded.
7. Unique sequence per entity.
8. Audit order preserved.

FAILURE CONTRACT

Failed mutation = zero audit.
Unauthorized mutation = zero audit.
Losing concurrent mutation = zero audit.

AI advisory analysis is not a human-attributed official mutation.

AI cannot generate an audit record pretending to be a human actor.
