STEP 7Y — HUMAN AUTHORIZATION MATRIX

GENERAL

Official safeguarding mutation requires attributable HUMAN actor.


ANY AUTHORIZED STAFF MEMBER

ALLOWED:
- report suspected abuse
- report suspected neglect
- report suspected exploitation
- report dignity concern
- report rights concern
- report privacy concern
- request immediate safeguarding review

No reporter is required to prove misconduct before reporting.


CAREGIVER / NURSE

ALLOWED:
- factual safeguarding report
- factual resident statement documentation
- factual observation documentation
- request urgent supervisor review

DENIED:
- final investigation finding
- disciplinary decision


SUPERVISOR / CARE MANAGER

ALLOWED:
- safeguarding triage
- authorize proportionate temporary protective measure
- assign responsible reviewer
- governance escalation
- initiate post-event review

Protective measure must be necessity-based and rights-aware.


AUTHORIZED SAFEGUARDING REVIEWER

ALLOWED:
- accept assigned safeguarding case
- conduct human review
- document evidence and uncertainty
- resolve safeguarding review within authority
- recommend further escalation

Reviewer may not fabricate certainty where evidence is incomplete.


SENIOR AUTHORIZED MANAGEMENT

ALLOWED where applicable:
- external reporting decision
- disciplinary process referral
- regulator / authority referral
- governance resolution

External legal reporting obligations are handled under applicable policy/law,
not autonomous AI decision.


AI / SYSTEM

AI = advisory only.
SYSTEM = infrastructure only.

DENIED:
- official safeguarding report creation
- official triage
- allegation substantiation
- perpetrator determination
- disciplinary action
- staff suspension
- staff termination
- resident-right restriction
- family-contact restriction
- police notification decision
- regulator notification decision
- external authority reporting decision
- protective action authorization
- safeguarding review resolution

HUMAN ACCOUNTABILITY = REQUIRED
