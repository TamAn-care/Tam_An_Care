STEP 7Z — HUMAN AUTHORIZATION MATRIX

GENERAL

Official Step 7Z mutation requires attributable HUMAN actor.


CAREGIVER

ALLOWED:
- report fall
- report near fall
- document observable facts
- request human review
- document transfer check where authorized by policy

DENIED:
- diagnose injury
- prescribe medication
- prescribe restraint
- decide hospital transfer


NURSE

ALLOWED:
- fall-risk assessment
- verify factual observation
- mobility support review within authorized scope
- post-fall factual assessment
- initiate human escalation

DENIED:
- autonomous medication mutation through Step 7Z
- autonomous restraint through Step 7Z


SUPERVISOR / CARE MANAGER

ALLOWED:
- review mobility support plan
- assign accountable fall-event owner
- authorize non-pharmacological prevention support
- governance escalation
- post-event review


AUTHORIZED CLINICAL PROFESSIONAL

Clinical diagnosis / treatment decisions follow the relevant clinical domain
and applicable professional scope.

Step 7Z does not duplicate those authorities.


AI / SYSTEM

AI = ADVISORY ONLY.
SYSTEM = INFRASTRUCTURE ONLY.

DENIED:
- official fall-risk verification
- injury diagnosis
- fracture determination
- head-injury determination
- restraint decision
- medication action
- hospital-transfer decision
- mobility restriction change
- assistive-device prescription change

- escalation resolution
