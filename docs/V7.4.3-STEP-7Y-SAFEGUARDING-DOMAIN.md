# Tam An Care V7.4.3 — Step 7Y
## Safeguarding / Abuse / Neglect / Exploitation / Resident Rights & Protection

### Purpose

Provide a human-accountable safeguarding framework for:

- suspected abuse reporting
- suspected neglect reporting
- suspected exploitation reporting
- dignity / rights concern reporting
- immediate safeguarding triage
- protective action governance
- accountable assignment
- safeguarding review
- escalation
- post-event review
- audit

### Core safeguarding principles

1. Suspicion is not a finding of guilt.
2. Reported concern is not proof of abuse.
3. AI pattern detection is not an allegation determination.
4. Resident dignity and rights must be preserved.
5. Privacy and need-to-know access are required.
6. Non-retaliation is mandatory.
7. Protective actions require attributable human authorization.
8. Investigative conclusions require authorized humans.
9. Disciplinary action remains outside AI authority.
10. External reporting decisions remain human-authorized.
11. Restriction of resident rights requires explicit human governance.
12. Emergency protective actions must remain human accountable.
13. Failed mutation creates zero business mutation and zero audit.
14. Incompatible concurrent mutations require single-winner semantics.

### Domain flow

Human Safeguarding Report
-> Human Safeguarding Triage
-> Optional Immediate Protective Action
-> Assignment
-> Acknowledgement
-> Review / Investigation
-> Optional Escalation
-> Resolution
-> Post-Event Review
-> Closure
-> Audit

### Explicitly prohibited autonomous behavior

AI / SYSTEM cannot:

- determine abuse occurred
- determine neglect occurred
- determine exploitation occurred
- identify perpetrator as fact
- impose disciplinary action
- suspend staff
- terminate staff
- restrict resident movement
- restrict resident communication
- restrict family contact
- decide police notification
- decide regulator notification
- decide emergency transfer
- create medication action
- create clinical treatment
- create incident autonomously
