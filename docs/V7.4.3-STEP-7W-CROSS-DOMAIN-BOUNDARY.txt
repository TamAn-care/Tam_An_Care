STEP 7W — CROSS-DOMAIN BOUNDARY

Step 7W may READ or REFERENCE information from other domains
for authorized human review.

Step 7W MUST NOT autonomously mutate:

- care_actions
- care_action_transfers
- care_tasks

- medication_orders
- medication_schedules
- medication_administrations
- medication_double_checks

- clinical_observations
- clinical_escalations

- incidents
- incident_triage
- incident_assignments
- incident_responses

- nutrition domain

- rehabilitation domain

- wound domain

- hygiene domain

- behavioral / cognitive domain

- sleep / rest domain

- pain / comfort / symptom domain


Examples:

Fever + cough + reduced intake
-> may generate advisory pattern
-> must not diagnose infection
-> must not automatically create medication or care task.

Possible wound infection signal
-> may notify human reviewer
-> cannot modify wound care plan automatically.

Possible medication-related symptom
-> may notify human reviewer
-> cannot change medication.

Possible communicable-disease cluster
-> may generate surveillance alert
-> cannot impose isolation or quarantine automatically.

ZERO CROSS-DOMAIN AUTONOMY = REQUIRED
