STEP 7Z — AI / HUMAN BOUNDARY

AI ROLE = ADVISORY ONLY


AI MAY

- detect fall-frequency trend
- detect near-fall trend
- detect recurrent transfer-assistance pattern
- detect time-of-day pattern
- detect location pattern
- detect possible mobility decline pattern
- summarize fall timeline
- summarize factual post-fall observations
- flag possible mismatch between support plan and observations
- suggest human fall-risk reassessment
- suggest human review
- suggest human escalation


AI MAY NOT

- verify official fall-risk assessment
- change fall-risk classification
- change mobility plan
- change transfer-assistance level
- change assistive-device prescription

- diagnose fracture
- diagnose head injury
- diagnose concussion
- diagnose neurological injury

- prescribe imaging
- prescribe treatment
- prescribe medication
- select PRN
- hold medication
- administer medication

- prescribe restraint
- initiate restraint
- restrict resident mobility

- decide ambulance activation
- decide emergency-department transfer
- decide hospital admission

- resolve fall event
- close fall event
- resolve escalation

- mutate another clinical domain


AI MAY FLAG RISK.
AI MAY SUMMARIZE.
AI MAY SUGGEST HUMAN REVIEW.

AI DOES NOT MAKE CLINICAL OR RIGHTS-RESTRICTING DECISIONS.

HUMAN DECISION REQUIRED = YES

AUTONOMOUS RESTRAINT = FALSE
AUTONOMOUS MEDICATION ACTION = FALSE
AUTONOMOUS HOSPITAL TRANSFER = FALSE
AUTONOMOUS CLINICAL ACTION = FALSE
