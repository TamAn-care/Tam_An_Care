# Tam An Care V7.4.3 — Step 7V
## Pain / Comfort / Symptom Management

### Purpose

Provide a human-accountable domain for:

- pain observation
- structured human pain assessment
- discomfort observation
- symptom observation
- comfort goals
- non-pharmacological comfort intervention
- reassessment
- safety alert
- escalation
- longitudinal audit

### Fundamental rules

1. Observable symptom is not diagnosis.
2. Pain score alone does not determine treatment.
3. Human context and resident communication ability matter.
4. Dementia or communication difficulty does not authorize
   autonomous inference of diagnosis.
5. Medication decisions remain outside Step 7V.
6. PRN medication execution remains in medication workflow.
7. AI is advisory only.
8. Official mutations require attributable human actors.
9. State mutation requires transaction + SELECT ... FOR UPDATE.
10. Incompatible concurrency is single-winner.
11. Failed mutation produces zero audit.
12. Cross-domain clinical action is never autonomous.

### Domain flow

Resident
-> Human Pain / Comfort Care Plan
-> Human Pain Assessment or Symptom Observation
-> Human Comfort Intervention
-> Human Reassessment
-> Optional Human-Confirmed Safety Alert
-> Optional Escalation
-> Human Review / Resolution
-> Audit

### Explicit exclusions

Step 7V does NOT autonomously:

- diagnose disease
- determine etiology of pain
- diagnose fracture
- diagnose infection
- diagnose myocardial infarction
- diagnose stroke
- diagnose delirium
- prescribe analgesic
- prescribe opioid
- select PRN medication
- change dose
- administer medication
- order restraint
- transfer resident
- call emergency service
- create official incident
- create care task
- create care action
