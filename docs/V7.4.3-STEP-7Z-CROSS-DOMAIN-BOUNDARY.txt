STEP 7Z — CROSS-DOMAIN BOUNDARY

Step 7Z may reference information from other domains.

It must not autonomously mutate:

- care actions
- care tasks
- medication
- clinical observations
- incidents
- nutrition
- rehabilitation
- wound management
- hygiene
- behavioral / dementia
- sleep
- pain / comfort
- infection
- medication safety
- safeguarding


Example:

Repeated night-time near falls
-> AI may flag trend
-> human review may consider sleep / mobility factors
-> Step 7Z cannot modify sleep plan automatically.

Possible dizziness around medication times
-> may trigger human review
-> Step 7Z cannot hold medication.

Possible injury after fall
-> may trigger human escalation
-> Step 7Z cannot diagnose injury.

ZERO CROSS-DOMAIN AUTONOMY = REQUIRED
