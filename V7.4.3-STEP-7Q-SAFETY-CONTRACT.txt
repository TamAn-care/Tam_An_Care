TAM AN CARE V7.4.3
STEP 7Q SAFETY CONTRACT

FAIL-CLOSED PRINCIPLE

A session must not proceed to READY / IN_PROGRESS
when an applicable documented safety restriction
has not been reviewed by an accountable human.


SAFETY INFORMATION MAY INCLUDE

mobility precaution
transfer precaution
weight-bearing restriction
assistive-device requirement
positioning precaution
supervision requirement
activity limitation
clinical restriction
post-fall restriction
other human-approved restriction


RESTRICTION GOVERNANCE

AI cannot remove, relax or override a restriction.

CAREGIVER cannot independently override
a documented clinical or rehabilitation restriction.

Any restriction change requires the
appropriate authorized human workflow.


NO HARDCODED TREATMENT THRESHOLDS

STEP 7Q does not hardcode:

therapy intensity
exercise progression
weight-bearing progression
clinical diagnosis
treatment threshold
emergency threshold

Approved human configuration / professional judgement
is required.


RESIDENT REFUSAL

Resident refusal is documented by a human.

AI cannot infer refusal and commit it
as an official resident record.


FALL / EMERGENCY BOUNDARY

An activity-related concern may raise
a human-review alert.

STEP 7Q must not automatically:

create an Incident,
declare a fall,
call emergency services,
alter medication,
change clinical treatment.

Explicit target-domain human action is required.


FUNCTIONAL DECLINE

AI or human observation may identify
possible decline.

Possible decline does not itself change:

rehabilitation plan,
exercise intensity,
mobility status,
transfer status,
medication,
clinical treatment.

Human review is required.
