TAM AN CARE V7.4.3
STEP 7N ABNORMAL FINDING / ESCALATION CONTRACT


PRINCIPLE

An abnormal measurement is a signal for
human attention.

It is NOT, by itself:

- a diagnosis
- a prescription
- a medication instruction
- a treatment order
- a Care Action


THRESHOLDS

Clinical thresholds are configuration.

Thresholds must not be invented by AI.

Threshold configuration requires:

- identifiable version
- approving human authority
- effective period
- units
- observation type
- applicable resident/context rules
- auditability


FLOW

Observation
-> threshold / pattern evaluation
-> possible abnormal signal
-> human acknowledgement
-> human review
-> human escalation if required
-> optional explicit Care Action link


AI

AI may raise:

POSSIBLE_ABNORMAL_TREND
POSSIBLE_DETERIORATION
REVIEW_RECOMMENDED

AI may not change:

abnormal finding lifecycle
escalation lifecycle
Care Action lifecycle
Medication lifecycle


CARE ACTION

A Care Action may only be linked or created through
a separately authorized human workflow.

Observation -> Care Action automatically = NO

Abnormal Finding -> Care Action automatically = NO

AI -> Care Action automatically = NO


CONCURRENCY

Two humans acknowledging/reviewing the same finding:

row lock required.

For an exclusive transition:

exactly one winner.

No duplicate escalation for the same exclusive
transition unless explicitly allowed by future design.
