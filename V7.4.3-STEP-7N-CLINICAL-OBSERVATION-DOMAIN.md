TAM AN CARE V7.4.3
STEP 7N — CLINICAL OBSERVATION / VITAL SIGNS / NURSING NOTES

PURPOSE

Provide an auditable human-controlled domain for:

Resident
-> Clinical Observation / Vital Sign
-> Nursing Note
-> Abnormal Finding
-> Human Review
-> Escalation
-> Optional Care Action linkage
-> Audit


SUPPORTED OBSERVATION EXAMPLES

The domain may store observations including:

- blood pressure
- pulse / heart rate
- temperature
- SpO2
- respiratory rate
- blood glucose when applicable
- body weight when applicable
- pain score when locally approved
- consciousness / orientation observations
- mobility / fall-related observation
- intake / output observation where applicable
- other configured clinical observations


IMPORTANT

The software SHALL NOT embed treatment decisions
inside an observation value.

Clinical threshold values SHALL NOT be hard-coded
by the AI layer.

Threshold profiles must be:

- configurable
- versioned
- approved by authorized humans
- resident/context aware where appropriate
- auditable


CLINICAL OBSERVATION PRINCIPLES

1. Observation records are attributable to a human recorder.

2. Measurements preserve:
   value
   unit
   measured_at
   recorded_at
   recorder identity
   measurement source

3. A signed / verified observation is not silently overwritten.

4. Correction occurs through an amendment record.

5. Original value remains recoverable.

6. Deletion of clinical history is not part of normal workflow.

7. Abnormal detection does not equal diagnosis.

8. Abnormal detection does not equal treatment instruction.

9. Escalation requires human accountability.

10. Care Action linkage is explicit and human-controlled.

11. No automatic conversion:
    Observation -> Care Action = NO

12. No automatic medication action.

13. No automatic diagnosis.

14. No autonomous clinical action.


CONCURRENCY

All mutating operations on an existing clinical
observation / finding / escalation must use:

SELECT FOR UPDATE

or equivalent database row locking.

Concurrent competing transitions:

exactly one applicable mutation wins.

Failed mutation:

ZERO business mutation
ZERO audit event.


AI

AI may:

- analyze trends
- detect possible anomalies
- highlight deterioration patterns
- rank observations for human attention
- generate non-binding alerts
- recommend human review

AI may not:

- diagnose
- prescribe
- change medication
- choose treatment
- sign a nursing note
- verify an observation
- close an abnormal finding
- approve escalation
- create a Care Action autonomously
- mutate the clinical record autonomously
