TAM AN CARE V7.4.3
STEP 7R — SKIN INTEGRITY / WOUND / PRESSURE INJURY MANAGEMENT

PURPOSE

Provide an auditable operational domain for human-managed skin integrity,
wound and pressure-injury care.

DOMAIN FLOW

Resident
-> Human Skin Assessment
-> Optional Wound Record
-> Human Classification / Review
-> Human Wound Care Plan
-> Human Assignment / Acknowledgement
-> Dressing / Treatment Record
-> Repositioning / Prevention Record
-> Progress Observation
-> Human Escalation when needed
-> Human Resolution
-> Human Closure
-> Audit

CORE PRINCIPLES

1. Every official clinical record is attributable to an authorized human.
2. AI may detect patterns, risks or anomalies only.
3. AI never creates an official wound diagnosis or classification.
4. AI never prescribes wound treatment or dressing protocol.
5. AI never changes medication.
6. AI never makes hospital-transfer or emergency decisions.
7. AI never resolves or closes a wound.
8. Verified clinical records are never silently overwritten.
9. Corrections use append-only amendment history.
10. Cross-domain actions require explicit authorized human action.
11. No autonomous Care Action creation.
12. No autonomous Care Task creation.
13. No autonomous Medication mutation.
14. No autonomous Incident creation.
15. No autonomous Clinical mutation.
16. autonomousClinicalAction = false.
