TAM AN CARE V7.4.3
STEP 7S SAFETY / DIGNITY CONTRACT

DIGNITY

1. Personal privacy is mandatory.
2. Resident dignity must be preserved.
3. Resident refusal must not be silently converted into completion.
4. Consent / cooperation must not be fabricated.
5. Personal-care records must be attributable to a human.


BATHING / TRANSFER / FALL SAFETY

6. Required mobility support must be available before care proceeds.
7. Required transfer support must be available before care proceeds.
8. Applicable fall precautions must be considered.
9. Unsafe conditions must fail closed or be escalated.
10. AI does not declare transfer safety.
11. AI does not declare bathing safety.


TOILETING / CONTINENCE SAFETY

12. Continence observation is factual observation, not autonomous diagnosis.
13. AI does not diagnose urinary infection.
14. AI does not diagnose bowel disease.
15. AI does not diagnose incontinence type.
16. AI does not prescribe continence medication.
17. AI does not prescribe bowel medication.
18. AI does not autonomously change hydration or nutrition plans.


SKIN SAFETY

19. Skin observation may be recorded factually by humans.
20. Suspected skin integrity problem may be explicitly escalated.
21. Personal-care domain does not autonomously create wound records.


FAILURE ATOMICITY

22. State mutation requires database transaction.
23. Competing state mutation requires row lock.
24. Failed mutation = zero business mutation.
25. Failed mutation = zero audit.
26. Losing concurrency request = zero mutation + zero audit.
27. Exactly one incompatible terminal state may win.
