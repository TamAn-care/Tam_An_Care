# TAM AN CARE V7.4.3
# STEP 8F.B — EXACT ACTOR / STAFF IDENTITY CONTRACT

## 1. Architectural decision

DECISION = CREATE_MINIMAL_CANONICAL_HUMAN_ACTOR_REGISTRY

REUSE_EXISTING_MODEL = NO

CANONICAL_TARGET = staff_actors

The current system contains actor identifiers and authorization
logic, but no canonical human operational identity source of truth.

resident_access_assignments remains an authorization-grant table.
It must not become the staff registry.

---

## 2. Canonical identity key

actor_id TEXT PRIMARY KEY

Rules:

- immutable
- opaque
- not derived from display name
- not derived from email
- not reused
- compatible with existing actorId strings

Recommended future generated form:

actor-<uuid>

## 4. Human operational roles

CAREGIVER
NURSE
SUPERVISOR
CARE_MANAGER

Role identity and resident authorization scope remain separate.

CAREGIVER may receive DIRECT_CARE.

NURSE may receive CLINICAL_CARE.

SUPERVISOR retains the Step 8D facility-wide active-resident policy.

CARE_MANAGER remains recognized for compatibility with existing
domain authorization semantics but receives no new resident scope
without a later explicit contract.

---

## 5. Non-human principals

AI
SYSTEM

AI and SYSTEM are not rows in staff_actors.

They receive no human operational authority from the human registry.

A future machine-principal registry, if required, must be separate.

---

## 6. Lifecycle

ACTIVE
INACTIVE
SUSPENDED
ARCHIVED

ACTIVE:
eligible for operational authorization subject to role and scope.

INACTIVE:
identity retained; authorization fails closed.

SUSPENDED:
temporary administrative/security block; authorization fails closed.

ARCHIVED:
historical identity only; no new operational authority.

---

## 7. Separation of responsibilities

staff_actors answers:
WHO is the human actor?

resident_access_assignments answers:
WHICH resident may the actor access?

domain authorization answers:
WHAT action may the actor perform?

These responsibilities must remain separate.

---

## 8. Step 8D integration

Future canonical enforcement must require:

1. actor exists in staff_actors
2. actor status = ACTIVE
3. claimed role matches canonical operational role
4. resident access policy is satisfied

CAREGIVER:
ACTIVE CAREGIVER
+ ACTIVE DIRECT_CARE assignment

NURSE:
ACTIVE NURSE
+ ACTIVE CLINICAL_CARE assignment

SUPERVISOR:
ACTIVE SUPERVISOR
+ existing Step 8D supervisory policy

Unknown, INACTIVE, SUSPENDED or ARCHIVED actor:
fail closed.

Unauthorized resident remains non-disclosing HTTP 404.

---

## 9. Step 8E integration

Resident-access administration eventually requires requesting
Supervisor to be:

- present in staff_actors
- ACTIVE
- canonical role SUPERVISOR

Assignment targets must also:

- exist in staff_actors
- be ACTIVE

Role / scope compatibility remains:

CAREGIVER -> DIRECT_CARE
NURSE -> CLINICAL_CARE

Step 8E must not become staff lifecycle administration.

---

## 10. Backward compatibility

Existing actorId and actorRole request contracts remain structurally
compatible.

Migration pattern:

existing actorId + actorRole
-> canonical identity resolution
-> existing authorization logic

No system-wide request API rewrite is authorized.

Role claims must no longer be trusted by themselves once canonical
identity enforcement becomes active.

---

## 11. Referential integrity strategy

Incremental only.

Wave 1:
create staff_actors and shared identity resolution.

Wave 3:
