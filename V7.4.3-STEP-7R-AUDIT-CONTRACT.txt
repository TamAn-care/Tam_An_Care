STEP 7R AUDIT CONTRACT

Every successful official state-changing mutation must create
exactly one corresponding audit event in the same database transaction.

Audit fields include:

audit_id
event_sequence
resident_id
aggregate_type
aggregate_id
event_type
actor_id
actor_role
previous_state
new_state
created_at


AUDIT PRINCIPLES

append-only
human attributable
ordered per aggregate
same-transaction business mutation + audit
no audit without successful business mutation
no successful state mutation without audit

Failed authorization:
zero business mutation
zero audit

Lost race:
zero business mutation
zero audit

AI advisory event may be logged separately,
but it must never masquerade as an official human clinical mutation.
