TAM AN CARE V7.4.3
STEP 7L — CARE TASK LIFECYCLE
DESIGN BASELINE

================================================
NORMAL LIFECYCLE
================================================

PLANNED
  |
  v
ASSIGNED
  |
  v
IN_PROGRESS
  |
  v
COMPLETED


================================================
EXCEPTION STATES
================================================

PLANNED
  -> CANCELLED

ASSIGNED
  -> MISSED
  -> SKIPPED
  -> CANCELLED

IN_PROGRESS
  -> COMPLETED


================================================
DESIGN PRINCIPLES
================================================

1. PLANNED does not mean a human has accepted responsibility.

2. ASSIGNED means a human assignment exists.

3. IN_PROGRESS requires explicit authorized human execution.

4. COMPLETED requires explicit authorized human completion.

5. System time passing alone must not falsely certify care completion.

6. MISSED semantics require separate design validation.

7. SKIPPED requires accountable human reason.

8. CANCELLED requires authorized human policy.

9. AI cannot perform state-changing transitions.

10. Concurrency-sensitive transitions require row-level locking
    or equivalent single-winner control.

================================================
NOT YET FINALIZED
================================================

The exact use of MISSED vs OVERDUE is intentionally not finalized.

The exact rules for:
- recurring task generation
- reassign after missed task
- restart after skipped task
- evidence requirements
- high-risk clinical tasks

must be designed before implementation.
