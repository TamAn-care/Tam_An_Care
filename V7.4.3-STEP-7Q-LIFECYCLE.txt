SESSION

SCHEDULED
-> ASSIGNED
-> ACCEPTED
-> READY
-> IN_PROGRESS
-> COMPLETED

Alternative terminal states:

MISSED
REFUSED
HELD
CANCELLED

Rules:

ASSIGNED:
must contain human assigned_to + assigned_role.

ACCEPTED:
assigned human owner accepts responsibility.

READY:
documented safety / restriction check has occurred.

IN_PROGRESS:
only after READY.

COMPLETED:
human owner records completion.

MISSED:
human records factual non-occurrence.

REFUSED:
human records resident refusal.

HELD:
human records explicit hold;
hold does not itself define treatment.

CANCELLED:
authorized human cancellation.

Exactly one terminal outcome may win.
