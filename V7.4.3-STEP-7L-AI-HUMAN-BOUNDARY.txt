================================================

================================================

ASSIGN HUMAN RESPONSIBILITY

ACCEPT RESPONSIBILITY

START CARE TASK

COMPLETE CARE TASK

MARK CLINICAL TASK SKIPPED

CANCEL CLINICAL TASK

CERTIFY HUMAN CARE WAS DELIVERED

FABRICATE CARE NOTE

FABRICATE COMPLETION EVIDENCE

================================================
HUMAN CONTROL
================================================

State-changing care execution requires
an accountable human actor.

Human actor identity must ultimately be bound to
authenticated authorization.

================================================
SYSTEM AUTOMATION
================================================

Permitted system automation may include:

- deterministic schedule generation
- deadline calculation
- reminders
- overdue flags
- non-clinical notification delivery

System automation must not be represented as
human care completion.
