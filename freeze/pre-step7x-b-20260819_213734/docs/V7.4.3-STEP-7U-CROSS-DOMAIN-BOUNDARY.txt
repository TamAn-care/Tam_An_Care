STEP 7U — CROSS-DOMAIN BOUNDARY

Step 7U may reference existing records for HUMAN review.

Step 7U MUST NOT autonomously create or mutate:

- care_actions
- care_action_transfers
- care_tasks
- medication_orders
- medication_schedules
- medication_administrations
- medication_double_checks
- clinical_observations
- clinical_escalations
- incidents
- incident_triage
- incident_assignments
- incident_responses
- nutrition_plans
- nutrition_intake_records
- rehabilitation_plans
- activity_sessions
- wound_records
- wound_care_plans
- hygiene_care_plans
- hygiene_schedules
- behavioral_episodes
- behavioral_escalations

Examples:

Night-time fall concern
-> AI/system may ALERT
-> HUMAN decides whether incident workflow is required.

Repeated waking
-> may trigger HUMAN review
-> cannot create diagnosis.

Possible medication-associated sleep change
-> may alert HUMAN
-> cannot modify medication.

Night wandering observation
-> may alert HUMAN
-> cannot create behavioral episode automatically.

ZERO CROSS-DOMAIN AUTONOMY = REQUIRED
