STEP 7X — AUDIT CONTRACT

Every successful official Step 7X governance mutation requires audit.

Audit must be:

- append-only
- attributable
- uniquely sequenced per entity
- committed in same transaction
- immutable history

Audit must preserve:

- actor identity
- actor role
- reviewer identity where applicable
- first checker identity
- second checker identity
- related medication order / schedule / administration reference
- event type
- event timestamp

Failed mutation = zero audit.

Unauthorized mutation = zero audit.

Same-person invalid independent check = zero audit.

Losing concurrent mutation = zero audit.

AI advisory output cannot impersonate a human reviewer or checker.
