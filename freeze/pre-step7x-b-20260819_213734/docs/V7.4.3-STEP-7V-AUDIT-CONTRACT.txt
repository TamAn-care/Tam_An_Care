STEP 7V — AUDIT CONTRACT

Successful official mutations require audit.

Audit must be:

- append-only
- attributable
- ordered
- uniquely sequenced per entity
- committed in the same transaction as business mutation

Failed mutation = zero audit.

Unauthorized mutation = zero audit.

Losing concurrent mutation = zero audit.

AI advisory output cannot be recorded as if authored by a human.

AI cannot impersonate human clinical accountability.
