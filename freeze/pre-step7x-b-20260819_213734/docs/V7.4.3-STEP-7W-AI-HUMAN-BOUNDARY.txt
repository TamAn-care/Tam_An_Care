STEP 7W — AI / HUMAN BOUNDARY

AI ROLE = ADVISORY ONLY


AI MAY

- analyze longitudinal symptom observations
- detect unusual symptom clusters
- detect change from baseline
- detect possible facility-level clustering
- summarize exposure history
- summarize monitoring trends
- suggest human infection-control review
- suggest human escalation review
- generate advisory surveillance signal


AI MAY NOT

- diagnose infection
- diagnose sepsis
- diagnose COVID-19
- diagnose influenza
- diagnose pneumonia
- diagnose urinary tract infection
- diagnose wound infection

- prescribe antibiotic
- prescribe antiviral
- select antimicrobial
- change medication dose
- discontinue medication
- administer medication

- confirm official infection
- activate isolation
- activate quarantine
- activate precaution
- discontinue precaution

- decide ambulance
- decide emergency transfer
- decide hospital referral

- verify official observation
- review official exposure
- resolve official alert
- assign escalation reviewer
- accept escalation
- resolve escalation

- create incident
- create care action
- create care task
- mutate another clinical domain


AI MAY ALERT.
AI MAY SUMMARIZE.
AI MAY DETECT PATTERNS.
AI MAY RECOMMEND HUMAN REVIEW.

AI MAY NOT EXECUTE CLINICAL OR INFECTION-CONTROL DECISIONS.

HUMAN DECISION REQUIRED = YES

AUTONOMOUS CLINICAL ACTION = FALSE
