STEP 7U — NIGHT-TIME SAFETY CONTRACT

SLEEP / REST PRINCIPLE

Sleep and rest are resident needs and preferences.

The system must not force:
- sleep
- wake-up
- unnecessary night disturbance

Night monitoring must be proportionate to the approved
human care plan and safety requirement.


OBSERVATION != DIAGNOSIS

Factual observations do not constitute diagnosis.

The domain does not diagnose:
- insomnia
- sleep apnea
- circadian rhythm disorder
- delirium
- dementia
- psychiatric illness


NIGHT-TIME FALL SAFETY

Increased night mobility may trigger human safety review.

It MUST NOT automatically trigger:
- restraint
- sedation
- confinement
- medication
- forced bed rest


MEDICATION SAFETY

This domain cannot:
- prescribe sleep medicine
- change medication
- administer PRN sedative
- recommend a medication as an executable order

Medication action requires the medication domain
and an authorized HUMAN workflow.


RESTRAINT / CONFINEMENT

No automatic:
- physical restraint
- chemical restraint
- bed restraint
- room confinement
- seclusion
- isolation


ACUTE CHANGE

Significant sudden sleep/rest pattern change may generate
a HUMAN review recommendation.

Potential causes remain for human clinical evaluation.

The software does not diagnose the cause.


FAIL-CLOSED

Unauthorized mutation:
zero business mutation
zero audit

Invalid state:
zero business mutation
zero audit

Failed safety prerequisite:
zero business mutation
zero audit


AUTONOMOUS CLINICAL ACTION = FALSE
