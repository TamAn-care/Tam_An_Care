STEP 7V — HUMAN AUTHORIZATION MATRIX

GENERAL

Official business mutation requires attributable HUMAN actor.


CAREGIVER

ALLOWED:
- record resident-reported pain/discomfort
- record factual observable symptoms
- record approved comfort support
- document response
- request human review
- create escalation request where policy allows

DENIED:
- diagnosis
- medication decision
- opioid decision
- dose decision
- PRN decision


NURSE

ALLOWED:
- perform human pain assessment
- verify assessment
- perform human symptom review
- reassess resident
- create human-confirmed safety alert
- escalate for further review

Medication action remains governed separately.


SUPERVISOR / CARE MANAGER

ALLOWED:
- review care plan
- activate care plan
- assign escalation reviewer
- governance review


AUTHORIZED HUMAN REVIEWER

ALLOWED:
- accept assigned escalation
- perform review
- resolve escalation
- document follow-up


AI / SYSTEM

AI = advisory only.
SYSTEM = infrastructure only.

DENIED:
- official pain assessment
- official symptom confirmation
- diagnosis
- medication selection
- PRN decision
- opioid decision
- dose change
- medication administration
- transfer decision
- emergency-service decision
- escalation resolution
- care action creation
- care task creation

HUMAN ACCOUNTABILITY = REQUIRED
