STEP 7Y — AUDIT CONTRACT

Every successful official safeguarding mutation requires audit.

Audit must be:

- append-only
- attributable
- immutable history
- uniquely sequenced per entity
- committed in same transaction

Audit must preserve where applicable:

- reporter identity
- actor identity
- owner identity
- reviewer identity
- protective-action authorizer
- escalation reviewer
- event type
- timestamp

Failed mutation = zero audit.

Unauthorized mutation = zero audit.

Wrong reviewer mutation = zero audit.

Losing concurrent mutation = zero audit.

AI advisory output cannot impersonate:
- reporter
- reviewer
- investigator
- decision-maker
