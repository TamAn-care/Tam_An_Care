STEP 7W — HUMAN AUTHORIZATION MATRIX

GENERAL

Official infection-domain mutation requires attributable HUMAN actor.


CAREGIVER

ALLOWED:
- record factual observable symptoms
- record resident report
- document possible exposure context
- perform assigned monitoring
- request nurse / supervisor review

DENIED:
- infection diagnosis
- antibiotic decision
- antiviral decision
- isolation decision
- transfer decision


NURSE

ALLOWED:
- verify infection observation
- review exposure
- create human-confirmed alert
- perform monitoring
- request / initiate escalation
- document approved precautions where policy permits

Medication decision remains separate.


SUPERVISOR / CARE MANAGER

ALLOWED:
- review infection-control plan
- activate infection-control plan
- authorize facility-level precaution workflow where within policy
- assign escalation reviewer
- governance review


AUTHORIZED HUMAN REVIEWER

ALLOWED:
- accept assigned escalation
- perform review
- resolve escalation
- document follow-up


AI / SYSTEM

AI = advisory only.
SYSTEM = infrastructure only.

DENIED:
- official diagnosis
- official infection confirmation
- official sepsis determination
- antibiotic selection
- antiviral selection
- medication dose change
- medication administration
- precaution activation
- precaution discontinuation
- isolation decision
- quarantine decision
- transfer decision
- emergency-service decision
- escalation resolution
- incident creation
- care action creation
- care task creation

HUMAN ACCOUNTABILITY = REQUIRED
