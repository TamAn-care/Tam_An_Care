STEP 7Y — SAFEGUARDING / RIGHTS SAFETY CONTRACT

REPORT != PROVEN FINDING

A safeguarding report is a concern requiring human review.

It does not establish:

- guilt
- abuse
- neglect
- exploitation
- perpetrator identity


DIGNITY / RIGHTS

Resident dignity must be preserved.

Resident rights must not be restricted automatically.

No automatic restriction of:

- movement
- communication
- family contact
- personal property
- privacy
- consent rights


NON-RETALIATION

Reporter retaliation = PROHIBITED.

Resident retaliation = PROHIBITED.

Witness retaliation = PROHIBITED.

Reporting a concern must not automatically create punitive action.


PROTECTIVE ACTION

Protective action requires human authorization.

Protective action must be:

- necessary
- proportionate
- documented
- reviewable
- time-limited where appropriate
- attributable


NO AUTONOMOUS DISCIPLINE

AI / SYSTEM cannot:

- suspend employee
- terminate employee
- impose disciplinary action
- label person as perpetrator


NO AUTONOMOUS EXTERNAL REPORTING

AI / SYSTEM cannot decide:

- police notification
- regulator notification
- authority referral
- family notification
- media communication


PRIVACY

Safeguarding information is sensitive.

Access must follow:

- least privilege
- need to know
- attributable access
- auditability


FAIL-CLOSED

Unauthorized mutation:
zero business mutation
zero audit

Wrong reviewer:
zero business mutation
zero audit

Invalid protective action:
zero business mutation
zero audit

AUTONOMOUS RIGHTS RESTRICTION = FALSE
AUTONOMOUS DISCIPLINARY ACTION = FALSE
AUTONOMOUS CLINICAL ACTION = FALSE
