STEP 7W — AUDIT CONTRACT

Every successful official mutation requires audit.

Audit must be:

- append-only
- human-attributable
- uniquely sequenced per entity
- committed in the same database transaction
- immutable history

Failed mutation = zero audit.

Unauthorized mutation = zero audit.

Wrong reviewer mutation = zero audit.

Losing concurrent mutation = zero audit.

AI advisory output cannot impersonate human clinical accountability.

AI-generated signal is not an official diagnosis.
