STEP 7X — AI / HUMAN BOUNDARY

AI ROLE = ADVISORY ONLY


AI MAY

- compare medication lists
- detect possible reconciliation discrepancy
- detect possible duplicate therapy signal
- detect possible interaction signal
- detect missed-dose pattern
- detect administration-exception trend
- summarize medication history
- summarize possible adverse-reaction observations
- identify records requiring human review
- suggest reconciliation review
- suggest medication-safety review


AI MAY NOT

- create medication order
- prescribe medication
- select medication
- select PRN medication
- decide PRN administration

- change dose
- change route
- change frequency
- discontinue medication
- resume medication
- clinically hold medication

- administer medication
- mark medication administered

- perform official reconciliation completion
- perform official medication-order review
- perform official medication safety check

- act as first high-risk checker
- act as second high-risk checker
- impersonate human checker

- diagnose adverse drug reaction
- autonomously resolve medication discrepancy

- create care task
- create care action
- create incident
- mutate another clinical domain


AI MAY ALERT.
AI MAY COMPARE.
AI MAY SUMMARIZE.
AI MAY DETECT POSSIBLE PATTERNS.
AI MAY RECOMMEND HUMAN REVIEW.

AI MAY NOT EXECUTE MEDICATION OR CLINICAL DECISIONS.

HUMAN DECISION REQUIRED = YES

AUTONOMOUS MEDICATION ACTION = FALSE
AUTONOMOUS CLINICAL ACTION = FALSE
