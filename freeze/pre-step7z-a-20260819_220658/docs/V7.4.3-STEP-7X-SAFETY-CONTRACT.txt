STEP 7X — MEDICATION SAFETY CONTRACT

GOVERNANCE != PRESCRIBING

Medication reconciliation, medication review,
safety checking and discrepancy documentation
do not independently modify medication_orders.


NO AUTONOMOUS PRESCRIBING

Step 7X cannot:

- create medication order
- select medication
- select PRN medication
- change dose
- change route
- change frequency
- discontinue medication
- restart medication
- clinically hold medication


NO AUTONOMOUS ADMINISTRATION

Step 7X cannot:

- administer medication
- mark medication administered
- fabricate administration record
- fabricate independent double-check


HIGH-RISK SAFETY

High-risk classification must come from approved human/policy configuration.

No hardcoded drug name, class, dose or threshold may automatically
produce clinical treatment action.

When independent check is required:

FIRST CHECKER != SECOND CHECKER

Both must be attributable humans.

AI / SYSTEM checker = DENIED.


ADVERSE REACTION SAFETY

Possible adverse medication reaction observation
is a factual human observation.

Observation != diagnosis.

The observation may request human review.

It cannot automatically stop, hold, resume or replace medication.


PRN SAFETY

AI cannot decide whether PRN medication should be administered.

A recorded symptom or pain score cannot autonomously trigger PRN administration.


FAIL-CLOSED

Unauthorized mutation:
zero business mutation
zero audit

Invalid lifecycle:
zero business mutation
zero audit

Duplicate incompatible mutation:
loser business mutation = zero
loser audit = zero

Same-person independent double-check:
zero business mutation
zero audit

AUTONOMOUS MEDICATION ACTION = FALSE
AUTONOMOUS CLINICAL ACTION = FALSE
