STEP 7Y — AI / HUMAN BOUNDARY

AI ROLE = ADVISORY ONLY


AI MAY

- detect unusual safeguarding-related patterns
- detect repeated unexplained event patterns
- detect possible neglect-risk pattern
- detect possible exploitation-risk pattern
- detect repeated rights / dignity concerns
- summarize safeguarding timeline
- summarize factual evidence references
- identify missing review steps
- suggest human safeguarding review
- suggest human escalation


AI MAY NOT

- create official safeguarding report
- determine abuse occurred
- determine neglect occurred
- determine exploitation occurred
- determine allegation substantiated

- identify perpetrator as fact
- accuse staff
- accuse family member
- assign culpability

- authorize protective action
- restrict resident movement
- restrict communication
- restrict family contact
- restrict resident rights

- suspend staff
- terminate staff
- impose discipline

- decide police notification
- decide regulator notification
- decide authority referral
- decide external reporting

- complete official investigation
- resolve safeguarding case
- close safeguarding case

- create medication action
- create care action
- create care task
- create incident automatically
- mutate another clinical domain


AI MAY ALERT.
AI MAY SUMMARIZE.
AI MAY DETECT POSSIBLE PATTERNS.
AI MAY RECOMMEND HUMAN REVIEW.

AI MAY NOT MAKE SAFEGUARDING FINDINGS OR RIGHTS-RESTRICTING DECISIONS.

HUMAN DECISION REQUIRED = YES

AUTONOMOUS RIGHTS RESTRICTION = FALSE
AUTONOMOUS DISCIPLINARY ACTION = FALSE
AUTONOMOUS CLINICAL ACTION = FALSE
