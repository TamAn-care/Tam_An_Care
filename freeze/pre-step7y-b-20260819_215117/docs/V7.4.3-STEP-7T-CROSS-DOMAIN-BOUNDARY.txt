STEP 7T — CROSS-DOMAIN BOUNDARY

Step 7T may reference information from other domains for human review.

It MUST NOT autonomously create or mutate:

- care_actions
- care_action_transfers
- care_tasks
- medication_orders
- medication_schedules
- medication_administrations
- medication_double_checks
- clinical_observations
- clinical_escalations
- incidents
- incident_triage
- incident_assignments
- incident_responses
- nutrition_plans
- diet_orders
- rehabilitation_plans
- activity_sessions
- wound_records
- wound_care_plans
- wound_treatment_records
- hygiene_care_plans
- hygiene_schedules

Any cross-domain action requires an explicit authorized human workflow.

Examples:

Behavioral concern
-> may recommend HUMAN clinical review
-> must not create clinical diagnosis.

Fall-like detected event
-> may alert HUMAN
-> must not create official incident automatically.

Possible medication-related behavioral change
-> may alert HUMAN
-> must not modify medication.

Wandering risk
-> may alert HUMAN
-> must not impose restraint/confinement automatically.

ZERO CROSS-DOMAIN AUTONOMY = REQUIRED
