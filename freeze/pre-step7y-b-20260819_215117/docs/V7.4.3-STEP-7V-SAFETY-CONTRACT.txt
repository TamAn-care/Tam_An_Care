STEP 7V — PAIN / COMFORT / SYMPTOM SAFETY CONTRACT

OBSERVATION != DIAGNOSIS

Pain or symptom documentation does not independently establish
a medical diagnosis.


PAIN SCORE SAFETY

A numeric pain score, when used, is descriptive information.

No hardcoded pain score automatically triggers:
- medication
- opioid
- dose increase
- hospital transfer
- emergency service
- restraint


NON-VERBAL RESIDENT SAFETY

Observed behavior may support human assessment.

The system must not autonomously infer:
- diagnosis
- cause of pain
- medication requirement


MEDICATION BOUNDARY

Step 7V cannot:

- prescribe analgesic
- select PRN medication
- prescribe opioid
- change dose
- administer medication
- discontinue medication

Medication execution requires authorized human workflow
inside the medication domain.


ACUTE / EMERGENCY BOUNDARY

Potentially concerning symptom patterns may produce an advisory
or human-confirmed alert.

The software does not autonomously diagnose or determine emergency
treatment.

Human escalation is required.


FAIL-CLOSED

Unauthorized mutation:
zero business mutation
zero audit

Invalid lifecycle transition:
zero business mutation
zero audit

Failed ownership / reviewer validation:
zero business mutation
zero audit


AUTONOMOUS CLINICAL ACTION = FALSE
