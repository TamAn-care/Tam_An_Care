STEP 7Y — CROSS-DOMAIN BOUNDARY

Step 7Y may reference other domain information for safeguarding review.

Step 7Y MUST NOT autonomously mutate:

- care_actions
- care_action_transfers
- care_tasks

- medication_orders
- medication_schedules
- medication_administrations
- medication_double_checks

- clinical_observations
- clinical_escalations

- incidents
- incident_triage
- incident_assignments

- nutrition domain
- rehabilitation domain
- wound domain
- hygiene domain
- behavioral / cognitive domain
- sleep / rest domain
- pain / comfort domain
- infection domain
- medication-safety domain


Examples:

Repeated unexplained bruising
-> may generate safeguarding advisory signal
-> cannot conclude abuse.

Repeated missed care tasks
-> may generate neglect-risk advisory signal
-> cannot conclude neglect.

Financial irregularity pattern
-> may generate exploitation-risk advisory signal
-> cannot accuse a person.

Resident distress around a particular interaction
-> may suggest human safeguarding review
-> cannot impose staff discipline.

ZERO CROSS-DOMAIN AUTONOMY = REQUIRED
