STEP 7T — AI / HUMAN BOUNDARY

AI ROLE = ADVISORY ONLY

ALLOWED AI FUNCTIONS

- trend analysis of recorded observations
- anomaly detection
- pattern detection
- possible trigger correlation
- repeated wandering-pattern alert
- sleep/activity pattern alert when source data exists
- change-from-baseline alert
- support-strategy suggestion for human consideration
- summary of existing records
- reminder that human review may be appropriate

AI OUTPUT MUST BE IDENTIFIED AS:
- advisory
- non-diagnostic
- requiring human review where action is considered

AI IS DENIED FROM

- dementia diagnosis
- delirium diagnosis
- psychiatric diagnosis
- cognitive-capacity determination
- legal-capacity determination
- official cognitive assessment
- official behavioral episode creation
- official wandering/elopement event creation
- official incident creation
- record verification
- support-plan activation
- assignment
- acknowledgement
- escalation acceptance
- escalation resolution
- episode resolution
- episode closure
- restraint decision
- chemical restraint decision
- physical restraint decision
- seclusion/isolation decision
- medication order
- medication dose change
- PRN medication decision
- medication administration
- sedation decision
- involuntary transfer decision
- emergency-service decision
- care-action creation
- care-task creation
- clinical-observation mutation
- cross-domain mutation

AI MAY ALERT.
AI MAY SUGGEST.
AI MAY SUMMARIZE.

AI MAY NOT DECIDE OR EXECUTE CLINICAL CARE.

HUMAN DECISION REQUIRED = YES
AUTONOMOUS CLINICAL ACTION = FALSE
