STEP 7U — AI / HUMAN BOUNDARY

AI ROLE = ADVISORY ONLY


AI MAY

- analyze sleep/rest patterns
- detect repeated waking pattern
- detect change from recorded baseline
- detect night-time activity trend
- summarize existing sleep observations
- generate advisory anomaly alert
- suggest that human review may be appropriate
- identify possible correlation for human consideration


AI MAY NOT

- diagnose insomnia
- diagnose sleep apnea
- diagnose circadian disorder
- diagnose delirium
- diagnose dementia
- diagnose psychiatric illness

- prescribe sleep medicine
- change medication
- decide PRN sedative
- administer medication
- decide sedation

- decide restraint
- decide bed restraint
- decide confinement
- decide seclusion
- decide isolation

- force bedtime
- force wake time

- create official sleep observation
- verify official sleep observation
- activate sleep care plan
- complete night monitoring check
- create official sleep event
- assign event owner
- acknowledge official event
- resolve event
- close event
- accept escalation
- resolve escalation

- create official incident
- create care action
- create care task
- mutate another clinical domain

AI MAY ALERT.
AI MAY SUMMARIZE.
AI MAY SUGGEST HUMAN REVIEW.

AI MAY NOT EXECUTE CLINICAL CARE.

HUMAN DECISION REQUIRED = YES

AUTONOMOUS CLINICAL ACTION = FALSE
