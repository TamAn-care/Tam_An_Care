STEP 7T — SAFETY / DIGNITY / RIGHTS CONTRACT

OBSERVATION SAFETY
- record observable facts
- avoid unsupported diagnostic labels
- distinguish observation from clinical diagnosis
- meaningful baseline change may trigger HUMAN review
- no hardcoded diagnosis threshold

DEMENTIA / DELIRIUM BOUNDARY
This domain does not diagnose dementia, delirium or psychiatric illness.
Suspected acute cognitive change may generate an advisory alert for
human review only.

WANDERING / ELOPEMENT SAFETY
- record observable event and location facts
- human accountability required
- safety response must remain attributable to humans
- no automatic confinement
- no automatic locking/restriction decision
- no automatic emergency-service decision

DIGNITY / AUTONOMY
- dignity must be preserved
- privacy must be preserved
- resident preferences must be considered
- least-restrictive support is the design principle
- behavior alone does not authorize coercive intervention

RESTRAINT / SECLUSION
This domain MUST NOT autonomously authorize:
- physical restraint
- chemical restraint
- seclusion
- isolation
- involuntary confinement

MEDICATION
No medication order, dose change, PRN decision,
sedation or administration mutation may be made by this domain.

CLINICAL REVIEW
Potential pain, infection, medication effect, dehydration,
constipation, acute illness or other reversible contributors
may be documented as concerns for HUMAN clinical review.
The software does not diagnose the cause.

FAIL-CLOSED
Unauthorized actor = zero business mutation + zero audit.
Invalid state = zero business mutation + zero audit.
Failed safety precondition = zero business mutation + zero audit.

AUTONOMOUS CLINICAL ACTION = FALSE
