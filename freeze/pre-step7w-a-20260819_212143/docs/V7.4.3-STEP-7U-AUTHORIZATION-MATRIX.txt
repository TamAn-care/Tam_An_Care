STEP 7U — HUMAN AUTHORIZATION MATRIX

GENERAL RULE

Official mutation requires attributable HUMAN actor.

CAREGIVER

ALLOWED:
- record factual sleep observation
- perform approved night monitoring
- record night support response
- acknowledge assigned sleep/rest event
- escalate concern

DENIED:
- clinical diagnosis
- medication prescription
- sedative decision
- restraint decision

NURSE

ALLOWED:
- verify factual observation
- human safety review
- escalation creation
- escalation review where assigned
- document clinical concern for further human review

SUPERVISOR / CARE MANAGER

ALLOWED:
- review sleep care plan
- activate approved sleep care plan
- assign event owner
- assign escalation reviewer
- governance review

AUTHORIZED HUMAN REVIEWER

ALLOWED:
- accept assigned escalation
- perform human review
- resolve escalation
- document follow-up

AI / SYSTEM

AI = advisory only.

DENIED FROM:
- official diagnosis

- assignment
- acknowledgement
- verification
- escalation acceptance
- escalation resolution
