STEP 7U — AMENDMENT / CORRECTION CONTRACT

Verified record overwrite = NO.

Correction requires append-only amendment.

Amendment must preserve:

- original record
- human actor
- actor role
- amendment reason
- amendment timestamp
- attributable history

Compatible append-only amendments may both persist.

Lost amendment history = NO.

Invalid amendment:
zero business mutation
zero audit

Failed amendment:
zero business mutation
zero audit

Original history preserved = YES
