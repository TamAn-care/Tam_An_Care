STEP 7V — CROSS-DOMAIN BOUNDARY

Step 7V may reference information from other domains for human review.

Step 7V MUST NOT autonomously mutate:

- care_actions
- care_action_transfers
- care_tasks

- medication_orders
- medication_schedules
- medication_administrations
- medication_double_checks

- clinical_observations
- clinical_escalations

- incidents
- incident_triage
- incident_assignments
- incident_responses

- nutrition domain

- rehabilitation domain

- wound domain

- hygiene domain

- behavioral / cognitive domain

- sleep / rest domain


Examples:

Pain after fall
-> may suggest HUMAN incident review
-> must not create incident automatically.

Pain during wound care
-> may alert HUMAN
-> must not modify wound treatment automatically.

Possible medication-related discomfort
-> may alert HUMAN
-> cannot change medication.

Severe new symptom
-> may recommend urgent HUMAN review
-> cannot autonomously decide transfer or emergency service.

ZERO CROSS-DOMAIN AUTONOMY = REQUIRED
