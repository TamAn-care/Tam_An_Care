STEP 7T — AMENDMENT / CORRECTION CONTRACT

Verified record overwrite = NO.

Corrections to verified observations, responses and reviews must:
- preserve original record
- be append-only
- identify human amendment actor
- contain amendment reason
- contain amendment timestamp
- preserve attributable history

Concurrent independent append-only amendments may both persist
when they do not represent incompatible state transitions.

Invalid amendment:
zero business mutation
zero audit

Failed amendment:
zero business mutation
zero audit

Original history preserved = YES
Lost amendment history = NO
