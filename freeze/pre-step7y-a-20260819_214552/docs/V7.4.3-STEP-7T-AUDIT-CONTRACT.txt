STEP 7T — AUDIT CONTRACT

Audit is mandatory for every successful official mutation.

REQUIREMENTS

1. Business mutation and audit event occur in the same DB transaction.
2. Audit is append-only.
3. Audit identifies:
   - resident
   - entity
   - event
   - actor
   - actor role
   - timestamp
   - sequence
4. Official mutation requires attributable human actor.
5. Failed mutation = zero audit.
6. Losing concurrent mutation = zero audit.
7. Sequence must be unique and ordered per entity history.
8. Owner/reviewer identity must remain historically attributable.
9. AI advisory output is not an official human mutation.
10. AI cannot generate a human-attributed audit event.

AUDIT SEQUENCE
- contiguous for committed domain mutations
- unique
- transactionally allocated
- no duplicate winning sequence
