# Step 7V — Canonical Data Model

Exactly eight tables are designed.

## 1. pain_care_plans

Human-authored pain/comfort support plan.

Lifecycle:
DRAFT -> REVIEWED -> ACTIVE -> RETIRED

## 2. pain_assessments

Structured human pain assessment.

Concepts include:

- resident_id
- assessed_at
- assessment_method
- self_report_available
- pain_location
- pain_character
- pain_score_if_applicable
- observed_pain_behaviors
- functional_impact
- state
- assessor
- verifier
- amendment linkage

Pain score is descriptive data, not an autonomous treatment rule.

## 3. symptom_observations

Factual observable symptom/discomfort record.

Examples:

- grimacing
- guarding
- verbalized discomfort
- new restlessness
- nausea observation
- breathlessness observation
- other resident-reported symptom

Observation is not diagnosis.

## 4. comfort_interventions

Append-only human non-pharmacological comfort intervention.

Examples:

- repositioning for comfort
- environmental adjustment
- reassurance
- rest support
- heat/cold only where explicitly allowed by human-approved plan
- supportive positioning
- relaxation support

Medication action is excluded.

## 5. pain_reassessments

Human reassessment after observation/intervention.

Preserves longitudinal change.

Verified reassessment overwrite = NO.

## 6. pain_safety_alerts

Human-confirmed safety concern.

Examples:

- significant change from baseline
- unresolved severe discomfort
- new functional impact
- symptom requiring human clinical review

AI detections remain advisory until human review.

## 7. pain_escalations

Human escalation governance.

Lifecycle:
OPEN -> ASSIGNED -> ACCEPTED -> RESOLVED

Reviewer identity is preserved.

## 8. pain_audit

Append-only audit history.

Requirements:

- same transaction as business mutation
- unique sequence per entity
- attributable human actor
- failed mutation = zero audit
- losing concurrent mutation = zero audit
