STEP 7V — AI / HUMAN BOUNDARY

AI ROLE = ADVISORY ONLY


AI MAY

- analyze longitudinal pain scores
- detect increased discomfort pattern
- detect change from recorded baseline
- summarize pain history
- summarize symptom observations
- identify correlation for human review
- alert on persistent unresolved documented discomfort
- suggest human reassessment


AI MAY NOT

- diagnose source of pain
- diagnose fracture
- diagnose infection
- diagnose myocardial infarction
- diagnose stroke
- diagnose delirium

- prescribe analgesic
- prescribe opioid
- select PRN medication
- change medication dose
- discontinue medication
- administer medication

- make transfer decision
- make emergency-service decision
- decide restraint
- decide treatment

- perform official pain assessment
- verify official assessment
- verify symptom observation
- verify comfort intervention
- perform official reassessment
- confirm official safety alert
- assign escalation reviewer
- accept escalation
- resolve escalation

- create official incident
- create care action
- create care task
- mutate another clinical domain


AI MAY ALERT.
AI MAY SUMMARIZE.
AI MAY SUGGEST HUMAN REVIEW.

AI MAY NOT EXECUTE CLINICAL CARE.

HUMAN DECISION REQUIRED = YES

AUTONOMOUS CLINICAL ACTION = FALSE
