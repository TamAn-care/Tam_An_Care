TAM AN CARE V7.4.3
STEP 7P NUTRITION / HYDRATION SAFETY CONTRACT

1. Allergy and restriction information is safety-critical.

2. Confirmed allergy / restriction must never be bypassed
   by AI recommendation.

3. Missing or conflicting safety information:
   fail closed for potentially unsafe diet mutation.

4. Fluid restriction:
   explicit accountable human authorization required.

5. AI cannot initiate, remove or modify fluid restriction.

6. Swallowing-related restriction:
   human-approved source required.

7. AI cannot determine safe feeding route.

8. AI cannot determine safe food texture as an official order.

9. Human meal assistance does not equal clinical authorization.

10. Intake documentation is observation, not diagnosis.

11. Low intake or hydration concern may generate an alert.

12. Alert does not automatically:
    - create medication;
    - create Care Action;
    - create Care Task;
    - change diet;
    - change fluid restriction;
    - initiate treatment.

13. Human escalation is required for official intervention.

14. Terminal meal outcome is exactly one of:
    COMPLETED
    MISSED
    REFUSED
    HELD
    CANCELLED

15. Failed mutation:
    zero business mutation
    +
    zero audit.

16. autonomousClinicalAction = false
