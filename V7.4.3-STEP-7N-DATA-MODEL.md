TAM AN CARE V7.4.3
STEP 7N DATA MODEL DESIGN


TABLE 1
clinical_observations

PURPOSE:
Store one attributable clinical observation.

PROPOSED FIELDS:

clinical_observation_id
resident_id
observation_code

observation_type
numeric_value
text_value
unit

measured_at
recorded_at

recorded_by
recorded_by_role

measurement_source
device_reference

status

verified_by
verified_by_role
verified_at

threshold_profile_reference
abnormal_flag

created_at
updated_at


STATUS:

RECORDED
VERIFIED
AMENDED
VOIDED


RULES:

resident FK required

exactly one meaningful value representation
must be present where applicable

measured_at required

human recorder required

verification requires authorized human

verified clinical content is never silently
overwritten

VOIDED means record retained but excluded from
normal clinical use

hard delete = NO

TABLE 2
clinical_observation_amendments

PURPOSE:
Preserve corrections without destroying original data.

FIELDS:

amendment_id
clinical_observation_id
resident_id

amendment_reason

previous_value
corrected_value

amended_by
amended_by_role
amended_at

created_at


RULES:

original observation remains

reason required

human actor required

AI amendment = denied

one amendment creates one audit event


------------------------------------------------

TABLE 3
nursing_notes

PURPOSE:
Human clinical/care narrative associated with resident.

FIELDS:

nursing_note_id
resident_id
note_code

note_type
note_text

status

authored_by
authored_by_role
authored_at

signed_by
signed_by_role
signed_at

supersedes_note_id

created_at
updated_at


STATUS:

DRAFT
SIGNED
AMENDED
VOIDED


RULES:

DRAFT may be edited by authorized human author.

SIGNED content is immutable.

Correction after SIGNED creates amendment/superseding note.

AI cannot sign.

AI-generated text, if ever surfaced as drafting assistance,
must remain clearly marked and must require human adoption.

TABLE 6
clinical_audit

PURPOSE:
Append-only clinical domain audit.

FIELDS:

audit_id
event_sequence

resident_id

clinical_observation_id nullable
nursing_note_id nullable
abnormal_finding_id nullable
clinical_escalation_id nullable

event_type

actor_id
actor_role

previous_state
new_state

created_at


RULES:

append only

event sequence required

exactly one audit for each successful mutation

zero audit for rejected mutation

no orphan audit
