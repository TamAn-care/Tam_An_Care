AUDIT REQUIREMENTS

Every successful mutation creates exactly one
corresponding audit event.

Failed authorization:
ZERO mutation
ZERO audit

Failed transition:
ZERO mutation
ZERO audit

Concurrency loser:
ZERO mutation
ZERO audit

Audit stores:

event_sequence
human actor
human role
previous_state
new_state
timestamp

High-risk administration requires successful
MED_ADMIN_DOUBLE_CHECKED before MED_ADMINISTERED.

Audit cannot be created by AI to represent
a clinical action AI did not perform.
