## 1. Domain purpose

Step 8E owns administration of the lifecycle of
canonical human-to-resident access assignments.

Canonical persistence remains:

`resident_access_assignments`

No second authorization table may be created.

---

## 2. Architectural separation

8E WRITES assignment lifecycle state.

8D INTERPRETS canonical assignment state.

8B and 8C CONSUME authorization decisions.

8A remains unchanged.

Administration logic must not be embedded into
care-view or dashboard services.

---

## 3. Human authority

SUPERVISOR:
- create CAREGIVER / DIRECT_CARE grants
- create NURSE / CLINICAL_CARE grants
- revoke ACTIVE grants
- inspect assignment administration

CAREGIVER:
- no assignment mutation authority

NURSE:
- no assignment mutation authority

AI:
- denied

SYSTEM:
- denied

No actor may self-escalate through a client-supplied
assigned_by or assigned_by_role field.

Those values are server-derived.

---

## 4. Lifecycle

Persisted status:

ACTIVE
REVOKED

Natural expiry is derived from:

effective_to <= NOW()

Expired is not a new persisted status.

REVOKED rows are immutable historical grants.

A new access period requires a new row.

Hard delete is denied.

---

## 5. Create contract

Proposed endpoint:

POST /api/operations/residents/:residentId/access-assignments

Client input:

actorId
actorRole
accessScope
effectiveFrom
effectiveTo optional

Server derives:

resident_access_assignment_id
status=ACTIVE
assignedBy
assignedByRole
assignedAt
createdAt
updatedAt

---

## 6. Revoke contract

Proposed endpoint:

POST /api/operations/access-assignments/:assignmentId/revoke

Client input:

revocationReason

Server derives:

status=REVOKED
revokedBy
revokedByRole
revokedAt
updatedAt

Generic PATCH and DELETE are denied.

---

## 7. Read administration contract

GET /api/operations/access-assignments

GET /api/operations/residents/:residentId/access-assignments

Reads must be bounded and deterministically ordered.

History must be bounded.

---

## 8. Consistency contract

Create uses one transaction.

Resident must exist and be active.

Role/scope pairing must remain:

CAREGIVER -> DIRECT_CARE
NURSE -> CLINICAL_CARE

Duplicate active grant is prevented by the canonical
partial unique index.

Concurrent duplicate attempts must produce at most
one successful active grant.

---

## 9. Revocation contract

Revocation updates only:

status='ACTIVE'

Exactly one current assignment may transition.

Revoked rows never transition back to ACTIVE.

Repeated revoke must have deterministic semantics.

---

## 10. Security

Only server-authorized SUPERVISOR may mutate.

CAREGIVER / NURSE mutation = denied.

AI / SYSTEM mutation = denied.

Client cannot supply authority metadata.

No authorization bypass in 8B or 8C.

---

## 11. Audit ownership

Canonical assignment row preserves:

assigned_by
assigned_by_role
assigned_at
revoked_by
revoked_by_role
revoked_at
revocation_reason
created_at
updated_at

No separate audit history table is required in
Step 8E initial implementation unless later discovery
proves an existing platform audit requirement.

---

## 12. Performance

Administration reads are bounded.

No N+1.

No unbounded history.

Mutation operations address a single resident or
single assignment.

No materialized authorization view.

No cache in initial 8E.

---

## 13. Rollback boundary

Pre-8E active Step 8D image must remain preserved
until final Step 8E closure.

8E must not change canonical Step 8D interpretation.

Rollback must restore application behavior without
deleting valid assignment data unless test fixtures
were created by acceptance harnesses.
