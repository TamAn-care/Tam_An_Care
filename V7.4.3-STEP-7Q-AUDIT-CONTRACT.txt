ATOMICITY

Business mutation and audit commit
inside the same database transaction.

If authorization or lifecycle validation fails:

business mutation = zero
audit = zero


SEQUENCE

Audit event_sequence is monotonic
per aggregate.

No duplicate sequence per:

aggregate_type + aggregate_id


ATTRIBUTION

Official audit contains:

actor_id
actor_role
previous_state
new_state
created_at

No unattributed official human mutation.


AI AUDIT

AI advisory analysis may be logged as advisory context.

AI advisory output must not appear as
human rehabilitation approval,
functional verification,
session completion,
resident refusal,
or escalation resolution.
