# TAM AN CARE V7.4.3
# STEP 7M — MEDICATION MANAGEMENT / MAR

## 1. PURPOSE

Medication Management / Medication Administration Record (MAR)
is a human-controlled medication operations domain.

The application SHALL support:

Resident
-> Medication Order
-> Medication Schedule
-> Administration Instance
-> Human Assignment
-> Human Acceptance
-> Administration / Exception
-> Audit

The system SHALL NOT prescribe medication.

Medication orders must originate from an identifiable human
prescriber / authorized clinical source outside autonomous AI control.

## 2. CORE DOMAIN OBJECTS

1. Medication Order
2. Medication Schedule
3. Medication Administration
4. Medication High-Risk Double Check
5. Medication Audit

## 3. MEDICATION ORDER

A Medication Order describes an authorized medication instruction.

Minimum conceptual fields:

medication_order_id
resident_id
order_code
medication_name
generic_name
strength
dose
dose_unit
route
frequency
instructions
indication
prescriber_name
prescriber_reference
prescribed_at
effective_from
effective_to
high_risk
double_check_required
status
verified_by
verified_by_role
verified_at
created_at
updated_at

Medication Order states:

DRAFT
VERIFIED
ACTIVE
SUSPENDED
COMPLETED
CANCELLED

AI may not create, verify, activate, suspend,
complete or cancel a Medication Order.

## 4. MEDICATION SCHEDULE

A schedule defines planned dose occurrences.

Minimum conceptual fields:

medication_schedule_id
medication_order_id
resident_id
schedule_code
scheduled_at
administration_window_start
administration_window_end
status
created_at
updated_at

Schedule states:

PLANNED
ACTIVE
SUSPENDED
COMPLETED
CANCELLED

Schedule generation may be mechanically derived
from an ACTIVE human-verified order.

Generation is administrative automation,
not autonomous clinical prescribing.

## 5. MEDICATION ADMINISTRATION

Each scheduled dose produces a distinct
Medication Administration instance.

States:

SCHEDULED
ASSIGNED
ACCEPTED
READY
ADMINISTERED
MISSED
REFUSED
HELD
CANCELLED

Terminal states:

ADMINISTERED
MISSED
REFUSED
HELD
CANCELLED

## 6. ASSIGNMENT

Only an authorized human supervisor / care manager
may assign a Medication Administration.

Initial assignment must be atomic:

assigned_to
assigned_role
assigned_at

AI or SYSTEM cannot be assignee.

## 7. ACCEPTANCE

The assigned human owner must explicitly accept
responsibility before administration.

accepted_at is required before administration.

Another caregiver cannot accept on behalf
of the assigned owner.

## 8. ADMINISTRATION

Administration requires:

human assigned owner
accepted responsibility
valid active Medication Order
valid scheduled administration
required double-check satisfied when applicable
administration note / outcome
human actor identity

ADMINISTERED must never be produced autonomously.

## 9. EXCEPTIONS

The MAR must explicitly represent:

MISSED
REFUSED
HELD
CANCELLED

Each requires:

human actor
reason
timestamp
audit record

HELD is a human clinical/operational decision.
AI may warn or recommend review but cannot place
or remove a medication hold autonomously.

## 10. HIGH-RISK DOUBLE CHECK

For a medication administration with
double_check_required = true:

a successful independent human double-check
must exist before ADMINISTERED.

The checker must not be the same actor
as the assigned administrator.

The checker must be an authorized human role.

AI and SYSTEM cannot satisfy double-check.

## 11. CONCURRENCY

All mutating operations require database row lock.

SELECT FOR UPDATE
-> NOT FOUND
-> AUTHORIZATION
-> TRANSITION VALIDATION
-> UPDATE
-> AUDIT

Exactly one mutation may win a concurrency race.

## 12. CARE TASK SEPARATION

Medication Administration is NOT a generic Care Task.

No autonomous Care Task -> Medication Administration conversion.

The domains may reference one another operationally,
but medication safety lifecycle remains independent.

## 13. AI BOUNDARY

AI MAY:

detect potential missed dose
detect timing anomaly
detect duplicate schedule risk
detect documented medication interaction risk
detect adherence trend
generate alerts
recommend human review

AI MUST NOT:

prescribe
change medication
change dosage
activate an order
suspend an order
assign medication administration
accept responsibility
perform double-check
record administered
record refused
record missed
place hold
remove hold
cancel administration

autonomousClinicalAction = false
