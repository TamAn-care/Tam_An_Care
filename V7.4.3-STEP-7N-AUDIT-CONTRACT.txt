AUDIT RULES

1. Audit is append-only.

2. Successful state mutation:
   exactly one corresponding audit event.

3. Rejected mutation:
   ZERO business mutation
   ZERO audit.

4. Audit records actor identity and role.

5. Audit stores previous_state and new_state.

6. Event sequence must be contiguous within
   the audited aggregate.

7. No orphan audit rows.

8. AI analytic alerts are not equivalent to
   human clinical audit events.

9. AI cannot impersonate a human audit actor.

10. Correction history remains reconstructable.
